Overview

To Be Named is a turn-based JRPG where a nameless knight ascends a tower filled with 17 different enemy types, traps, roaming threats, and loot. The player improves their character over time by unlocking new attacks and using items while surviving increasingly dangerous encounters.

What I Did

I worked on the UI design and its functionality, ensuring clear and usable interfaces for combat, progression, and in-game systems. I also contributed to level design, helping structure how encounters, traps, and exploration were distributed throughout the tower to maintain pacing and tension. In addition, I collaborated on the character progression system (level-up system), supporting the implementation of how the player improves stats and abilities as they gain experience.

What I Learned

I learned how to design and implement functional game interfaces that support clarity and gameplay flow in a turn-based RPG. Working on level design helped me understand how to structure encounters and environmental challenges to control pacing and difficulty. I also gained experience with RPG progression systems and how they affect player motivation and balance. On a technical level, I improved my skills working with Godot, becoming more comfortable implementing UI and gameplay systems inside the engine and understanding how different systems connect within a full project.
