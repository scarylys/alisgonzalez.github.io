Overview

Luma is a narrative-driven 3D Semi-open world adventure that combines exploration, combat, and emotional storytelling. Players control a small moth navigating a dreamlike, fragmented archipelago affected by a mysterious corruption caused by the loss of lunar light. The game features interconnected islands that can be freely explored, encouraging discovery, environmental puzzles, and combat encounters. Progression is tied to restoring light across the world, both mechanically and symbolically. At its core, Luma delivers an emotionally driven experience where gameplay and narrative are closely intertwined, with each area representing different stages of grief and personal growth.

What I Did

I led the game design and developed most of the core systems, including combat mechanics, enemy behaviors, boss encounters, player abilities, and progression. I designed the core gameplay loop around exploration, combat, and light collection, and structured the open world into interconnected islands with a clear progression system. I also defined the difficulty curve, pacing, and player progression through abilities, economy, and collectibles. While I had some support in specific areas, I was responsible for the majority of design decisions and implementation.

What I Learned

I developed a deeper understanding of balancing interconnected gameplay systems within an Semi-open world structure. This included designing and tuning economy systems (shop, currency, and upgrades), ensuring that progression felt rewarding without breaking game balance. I also worked extensively on pacing, learning how to distribute combat, exploration, and narrative beats to maintain player engagement across a non-linear world. Refining game feel was a key part of the process, especially when iterating on combat responsiveness, player movement, and feedback systems. Overall, I gained hands-on experience in system balancing, player progression, and the challenges of maintaining consistency and clarity in a semi-open world design.
