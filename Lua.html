Overview

Lúa is a 2D side-scroller platform game developed during Global Game Jam 2025 in collaboration with Under Castle and Andrés Piñeiro. Inspired by Celeste and Ori and the Blind Forest, the game follows Ajo, a curious axolotl, who discovers a bubble at the bottom of the sea and must protect it while bringing it safely to the surface. Built around precision platforming and environmental tension, the project aimed to create a short but memorable challenge around the theme “Bubble.”

What I Did

My main contribution was designing the level flow and gameplay experience. I focused on creating engaging platforming sections that balanced difficulty and rhythm, using movement mechanics such as jumping, double jumping, and wall grabbing to keep progression dynamic and rewarding.

One of the main challenges was making the level design entertaining within a very short game format, ensuring that each section felt meaningful and maintained player motivation. I also worked on developing the project in 2D using Unreal Engine, adapting the engine’s tools to support responsive movement, collision systems, and a clear side-scrolling experience.

Level Design Layout

This blockout layout was used to plan the pacing, jumps, and enemy placement before polishing the final level.

What I Learned

I learned a lot about working in 2D with Unreal Engine, since it is mostly made for 3D games. It helped me understand better how to handle movement, collisions, camera setup, and level building in a different way. I also learned how important level design is, especially in a short platformer where every part of the level needs to feel interesting and well-paced.
