Overview

Lúa is a 2D side-scrolling platform game developed during Global Game Jam 2025 in collaboration with Under Castle and Andrés Piñeiro. Inspired by Celeste and Ori and the Blind Forest, it follows Ajo, a curious axolotl who discovers a bubble at the bottom of the sea and must protect it while bringing it safely to the surface. The game focuses on precision platforming and the theme “Bubble,” aiming to deliver a short but memorable experience.

What I Did

I contributed by designing both the game mechanics and the level flow, focusing on creating engaging platforming sections with a good balance of challenge and rhythm. I used movement mechanics such as jumping, double jumping, and wall grabbing to shape a dynamic and rewarding progression throughout the levels.

Level Design Layout

What I Learned

I learned a lot about working in 2D with Unreal Engine, which is mainly designed for 3D games. It helped me understand how to adapt collisions, camera setup, and level building for a 2D environment. I also gained a better understanding of level design in practice, especially in a short platformer where pacing and clarity are important throughout the experience.
